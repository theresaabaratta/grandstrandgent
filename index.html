Know Before You Book

Some groups should pass. Honestly.

The Gent would rather turn down a booking than show up to a room full of folded arms. Read both lists with your group before you reach out.

Book the Gent if your group…

  • Wants to be in the show, not beside it
  • Has a bride or birthday girl ready for the spotlight
  • Will cheer, dare and get up when called
  • Came to the Grand Strand to make a story
  • Is loud, bold and all-in from the first song

Pass if your group…

  • Only wants to sit back and watch
  • Has half the crew who'd rather not participate
  • Is looking for a stage show from across the room
  • Wants something mild enough for the in-laws
  • Needs a quiet, low-key night in

Quick Check
