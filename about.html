About

Meet the Gent.

Good manners, bad intentions and a firm belief that the best nights are the ones you're part of.

Why the Gent does it differently

The Gent started with a simple observation: the groups that had the most fun were never the ones sitting politely in a row. They were the ones laughing, daring each other and jumping in. So he built a show around that energy and stopped taking the bookings that didn't have it.

Every night is shaped by the room. He shows up sharp, takes the lead and turns a living room into the best part of the trip. The only thing he asks is that your group meets him halfway.

That's why he'll tell you to pass if you just want to watch. It isn't attitude. It's how he protects the night for the groups who want the real thing.

The Gent Code
